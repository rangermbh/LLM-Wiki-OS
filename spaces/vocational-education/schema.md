# Vocational Education Domain Schema

## Scope

Long-term knowledge related to vocational education, including theory, policy, educational reform, talent cultivation, curriculum development, AI in vocational education, and related research.

## Directory Map

```
vocational-education/
├── schema.md         # This file — domain definition
├── index.md          # Knowledge index
├── log.md            # Change log
```

## Topics

- Vocational education theory and philosophy
- Education policy and reform (China and international)
- Talent cultivation models and skilled workforce development
- Curriculum design and teaching methodology
- Industry-education integration (产教融合)
- Teacher development and qualification standards
- Practical training and apprenticeship systems
- AI and technology in vocational education
- Lifelong learning and professional development
